Allocute

The guideline range, worked out on the page, with every step cited.

Allocute is an iPhone app for people who work with the federal sentencing guidelines: defense attorneys, prosecutors, probation officers, law clerks and students. Pick the Chapter Two guideline, mark the specific offense characteristics that apply in the manual's own words, add the Chapter Three adjustments and the criminal history, and read the range: 63–78 months, Zone D.

What it does

  • The Sentencing Table, to touch: level and category give the range and the zone, and a statutory minimum or maximum narrows it under §5G1.1.
  • The Guidelines Manual in effect, searchable by word, section or statute.
  • A worksheet that follows §1B1.1: base offense level, specific offense characteristics, victim, role and obstruction adjustments, multiple counts, acceptance of responsibility, criminal history points, career offender and the other Chapter Four rules.
  • Saved cases, two scenarios side by side, and the worksheet as a PDF.

What it is not

Allocute is not legal advice and does not replace the manual, the statutes or the case law of your circuit. It is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the United States Sentencing Commission, the courts or any government body. The text of the manual is the Commission's, a work of the federal government; the official version is at ussc.gov.
